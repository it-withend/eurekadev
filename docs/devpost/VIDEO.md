# Demo video — 2-minute script

Record the phone screen (iPhone: Control Centre → Screen Recording; Android: quick
settings → Screen record) plus one short clip filmed from the side of the real
pendulum. Edit in CapCut / Clipchamp, upload to YouTube as **Unlisted**, paste the
link into Devpost. Speak calmly; subtitles help judges who watch muted.

| Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Phone hanging on a string by a doorframe, swinging | "This is a phone on a shoelace. In two minutes it will measure gravity to within one percent." |
| 0:10–0:25 | Home page with the live trace, then the experiment list | "Labs are expensive, but every student already carries an accelerometer, a gyroscope and a microphone. PocketLab turns them into twelve real physics experiments — in the browser, no install." |
| 0:25–0:45 | Pendulum: Set up step → Measure → tap Start, phone swings, beeps | "Pick an experiment and it tells you exactly what to do. Tap start, let go — the phone beeps and talks, because you can't read a screen that is swinging." |
| 0:45–1:05 | Result: g ± uncertainty, green verdict, switch "In simple words" ↔ "With formulas" | "You get gravity with an honest uncertainty and a fair verdict. Then the explanation — in simple words with your own numbers, or with the full formulas." |
| 1:05–1:20 | Ask the AI tutor a question (e.g. in Russian), answer appears | "Still unclear? The AI tutor sees your numbers and answers in any language — and it guides you instead of giving away answers." |
| 1:20–1:35 | Virtual lab: drag to the Moon, run, period ≈ 5 s | "No string at hand? Practise in the virtual lab — even on the Moon. The simulated data goes through exactly the same analysis." |
| 1:35–1:50 | Builder: type `4π²m/T²`, then the false-alarm chart | "Students can design their own experiments with a textbook formula. And we tested it hard: 360 correctly done experiments on simulated iPhones and Androids — 99.7 % are told they agree." |
| 1:50–2:00 | Thumbnail / link eurekadev.vercel.app | "PocketLab: your phone is a physics lab. Try it at eurekadev.vercel.app." |

Tips borrowed from the strongest gallery projects: the first 10 seconds show the
real thing (not a title card), every claim is shown on screen, and the video ends
on the link.
